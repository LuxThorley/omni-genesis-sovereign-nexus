IV–VI. Science, food, adjuncts

First-line legal tools: caffeine + L-theanine for clean attention; creatine monohydrate 3–5 g/day as an ATP buffer; EPA/DHA if the diet is low-fish. Kitchen first: oily fish or algal DHA, eggs, berries, greens, tea, modest coffee, ferments, olive oil, water.

Second-line, slow: bacopa, ashwagandha. Optional and quality-chaotic: lion’s mane, citicoline, rhodiola. Repair when measured low: vitamin D, B12, magnesium, iron.

Not recommended as consumer elixirs: unlicensed racetams and research chemicals, grey-market peptides, nicotine as a focus hack, alcohol as muse, cannabis for coding, prescription stimulants without a lawful prescriber, and self-administered psychedelics. No methods are given.

VII–IX. Mind, philosophy, correspondence

A calm prefrontal cortex is the best API. Fluency without doubt is capture. Water, tea, creatine, fish oil, older gardens, lion’s mane, breath and darkness may be read as symbols. Correspondence is not causation. Do not swallow a metaphor.
